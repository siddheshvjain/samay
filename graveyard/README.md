# The Graveyard

Strategies that failed. Honestly documented.

Failure is data. A failed strategy teaches more than a lucky winner.

## Contributing a Failure

Add a file: `graveyard/{title}.md`

```markdown
---
title: Strategy Name That Failed
author: your_github_username
date: 2025-01-01
cause_of_death: "Why it broke"
---

## What it was
Description...

## Why it looked good
Backtest stats...

## Why it failed
What changed...

## Post-mortem
Analysis...

## Lesson
What we learned...
```

## Why a Graveyard?

1. **Honors the failures** — Better than pretending they never existed.
2. **Teaches others** — "Don't go down this path" is valuable.
3. **Prevents rework** — If someone tried this and documented why it failed, the next person doesn't waste effort.
4. **Builds trust** — A community that admits failures is more credible than one that only publishes winners.

*The best researchers document the corpses on the battlefield.*
